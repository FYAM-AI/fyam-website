This policy explains how FYAM LTD ("FYAM", "we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, book a demo, receive an email from us or try our Smart Summarise demo.

FYAM LTD is a private limited company registered in England, company number 17325384. Our registered office is 27 Whitmore Road, Birmingham, B10 0NR. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Contact us about anything in this policy at info@fyam.co.uk.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We use a small number of service providers who process information on our behalf:

  • Microsoft 365: email.
  • Calendly: demo booking.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this website.
  • Cloudflare: running the demo service.
  • OpenAI: the AI models used by the demo. OpenAI's business terms state that information sent through its API is not used to train its models.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law allows, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

4. The Smart Summarise demo

Please do not upload or enter real information about people you support, staff or anyone else in the demo. Use the fictional sample documents provided, or information you have fully anonymised.

Smart Summarise is currently at pilot stage. Before any care provider uses it with real personal information, we will put a data processing agreement in place with that provider, support their data protection impact assessment (DPIA), and tell them exactly where their information is stored and processed. In that arrangement the care provider is the controller and FYAM acts as its processor.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiry and demo booking details: up to 2 years from our last contact with you, unless you become a customer.
  • Outreach records: we keep a record of who we have emailed and any opt-out, so that we never contact someone who has asked us not to.
  • Demo submissions: kept only as long as needed to run the demo, normally no more than 30 days.

6. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it (including to opt out of our emails at any time), and to have it transferred to another organisation. To use any of these rights, email info@fyam.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

We will update this page if anything changes and show the date of the latest version at the top.
